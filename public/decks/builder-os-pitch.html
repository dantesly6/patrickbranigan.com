Workweek · Builder Stack

Most teams keep strategy docs, design systems, and engineering standards in different tools, read by different people, enforced by no one.

What if it were all one operating system any teammate could just talk to?

The shape

Two stages, one operating system.

One layer answers what to build against live company data. The next answers how to build it well with our design system, architecture, and review loop. A builder talks to it in plain English; the system runs the rest.
