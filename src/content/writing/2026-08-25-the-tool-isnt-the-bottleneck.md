---
title: The tool isn't the bottleneck
date: 2026-08-25
excerpt: The tool gets better once you do
---

Over the past three to six months I've been asked a lot, by designers, engineers, product and business people alike, "how do I become a better builder?" My answer has changed as my own experience with building has evolved. Where I've landed most recently:

Give yourself some time to actually understand the fundamentals your concept calls for.

Nearly everyone who's asked me this is really asking a narrower question, motivated by a specific tool: "how do I use [insert tool] to [output] better representations of what's in my head?"

But if you give yourself a little time to deepen your understanding of the facets required to actualize what's in your head, your relationship with that tool grows on its own. Take it upon yourself to even slightly better understand the law of proximity, scale, and typography fundamentals, and the UI your tool outputs will get better by default, because you can now have a richer conversation with it.

Tools change. Workflows evolve. Fundamentals, historically, have not.

That's a design-specific example, but it holds for any facet. It's helped me. I believe it'll help you too.
