---
title: Steve was right
date: 2026-08-13
excerpt: We're all just beginners again
---

A statement Steve Jobs made at his commencement address at Stanford:  
  
"The lightness of being a beginner again, less sure about everything...freed me to enter one of the most creative periods of my life."  
  
Today many of us are finding ourselves in this exact circumstance. A beginner of sorts. Confronted with the unknown. Even if we can't see ourselves in the position, we are snugly between an era of old and an era of new when it comes to product, design and engineering. No one's going to tell you to "be a beginner." That's on you.  
  
There have been more days in the past six months where I've thought, "I have no idea what the fuck I'm doing." The truth is, it's okay.  
  
I've never felt more like a beginner again :)
